## Content requiring verification before publication (consolidated)
- Any "track record / years of expertise / top-rated / industry-leading" phrasing.
- Battery product specifics: capacities, brands, prices, stock, datasheets.
- BESS & Smart Grid Transformer technical specifications (ratings, voltages, efficiency, warranties, certifications).
- Announcement full body text (only titles + dates confirmed).
- Legal/policy full wording (Privacy, Cookie, Terms), Health & Safety formal policy, Legal Department scope.
- Actual cookies used in production (enable only categories genuinely used).
- ISO 9001 / any certifications, awards, project counts, client counts, testimonials — none fabricated.

## Intentionally excluded (with reason)
- Wix layout/structure and repeated per-page contact blocks — replaced by shared components.
- Legacy accessibility instructions referring to old browser behaviour — obsolete for the new site.
- Rotating hero slogans — replaced by one strong hero per UX research.
